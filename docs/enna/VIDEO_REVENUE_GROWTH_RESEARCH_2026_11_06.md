## Publishing experiments and measurement
- Stage 1: clear missing near-term images and avoid changing published or uncertain posts.
- Stage 2: after manual approval, publish max two original vertical tests per week on connected Facebook and Instagram, varied format, not cloned low-effort reposts.
- Stage 3: monitor same-platform 24h / 72h views, 3s hold / completion / average watch time if available, shares/saves, profile visits, aggregate resource clicks, owner-verified orders and payout receipts. Missing metric must stay null, not zero.
- Stage 4: wait for at least three verified videos per format and comparable same-platform baseline before promoting a format; no fabricated cause/effect claims.
- Stage 5: only then test one vetted affiliate listing or voluntary interest in a digital kit. No automated external publication or payment without approval.

## Quality, ethics and legal checklist
- Vietnamese text must render correctly at mobile resolution, original brand mascot and visual composition, source/rights for music, strong human-reviewed story, no watermarks from rivals.
- Do not rip videos, mass-generate repetitive content, fabricate user product reviews or claim personal use without testing. Meta explicitly deprioritizes minor edits/reuploads.
- Distinguish promotional content and disclose affiliate relationships. Check Vietnamese Advertising Law 75/2025/QH15 (effective Jan 1, 2026) and Decree 342/2025/NĐ-CP (effective Feb 15, 2026) as applied to concrete campaigns.
- Maintain campaign consent, opt-in audience metrics, provider confirmation, owner-approved contract and reconciliation. No false bank/payout/approval statements.
