## 4. 14-day controlled editorial experiment (proposed, not a claim of pre-existing schedule)

Use existing slots when appropriate; no flooding, no duplicate posts, no new scheduling from this research document.

- **Windows (25%)**: one *new* shortcut or workflow per post. Distinct lanes: window layout, virtual desktops, File Explorer, multi-monitor, accessibility, input tools. 30-day cooldown on previously posted combinations.
- **Excel (20%)**: Ctrl+T table, Alt+= total, Ctrl+Shift+L filter, Ctrl+E Flash Fill, IFERROR / basic dynamic arrays as separate, tested concepts (not all shortcuts).
- **PowerPoint (15%)**: Shift+F5, slide reuse, master/layout, align shapes, presenter tools. Explain version/OS caveats.
- **Other office productivity (15%)**: sensible filenames, meeting checklists, inbox hygiene, workflow tips.
- **Người Tròn office comics (15%)**: doodle-character visual humor with a sharp punchline. Avoid rigid AI posters or a repetitive three-box layout.
- **Stories/conversation (10%)**: fewer, stronger social dilemmas; measure authentic replies rather than vanity reach.

Each idea should have its own comedic situation and verified guidance. For Facebook/Instagram, original Người Tròn artwork is expected when publishing visual-first tips. The cat reaction photo remains an approved exception.

### Test design and decision rules
1. For each network, measure **24h / 72h / 7-day** post metrics. Keep post age explicit; compare like-for-like formats and roughly similar posting windows.
2. On Threads compare **useful short text** vs **useful short text + relevant illustration**, rather than presuming images cause improvement.
3. On Instagram evaluate swipe-friendly 3–5 slide tips by saves, shares, reach once a sample of actual published posts exists. FB evaluate real reach, reactions, comments and shares, and any paid exposure separately.
4. Require at least **5 actually published observations per test family** and at least 7 days before a format-level conclusion; rare outliers do not warrant redesigning everything.
5. Avoid repeatedly using the same Windows combinations, identical CTA or identical doodle framing.
6. **No fabricated conversion or ROI:** +15 daily followers are not known to come from one shortcut post. Affiliate monetization cannot be inferred from views.
7. For the 1,000 follower by Nov 9 milestone, the last verified 05 Nov count is **21 (2.1% of target)**; target remains highly at risk unless growth changes materially.
