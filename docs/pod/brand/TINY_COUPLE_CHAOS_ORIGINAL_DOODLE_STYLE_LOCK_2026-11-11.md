### Identity that MUST stay constant across ALL seasons and media
- Left-side figure when shown in the original: a small human-like doodle with brown hair in a messy high bun, large slightly mismatched round eyes, rosy cheeks, simple brick-red loose shirt.
- Other figure: black spiky hair, oversized quirky eyes, rosy cheeks, muted blue loose shirt.
- Original short/squat imperfect proportions; hand-drawn charcoal outline with intentionally uneven stroke width, rough contours and off-register texture, small simplified limbs, sparse thin handwritten lettering.
- Restrained original palette: warm off-white/beige on mockups, muted red/rust/brick, dusty/slate blue, charcoal black, soft skin peach and warm brown.
- Humor is self-aware, gentle and a bit awkward. Maintain generous negative space.
- **DO NOT** tidy, beautify, vector-smooth, polish, recolor, re-light, 3D-render, re-invent eyes or hairstyles, add glossy effects, make them more symmetrical, or re-render in a different mainstream cartoon style.

### What CAN change
Only surrounding **props, environment, clothing accessories on top of unchanged outfits if necessary (e.g. scarf), palette of temporary scene decorations, seasonal themes, text jokes, and context**. Examples: Xmas string lights/cocoa/presents; Valentine's awkward hearts/tiny notes; birthdays/bent candle; moving in/cardboard boxes; work-from-home laptop/deadline board; weekend groceries, blanket, couch.
No permanent costume/eye/hair/body changes; if in doubt leave the original artwork untouched and build the scene around it.

## 2. INSTAGRAM COMICS: INDEPENDENT SHORT EPISODES
**NO character names. NO fixed ongoing storyline. NO named Ari & Ren references.**
One self-contained couple moment per post. Reactions and actions can swap between partners; nobody is always the planner, clumsy person, or smart one.

**Default comic format:** 3–4 panels; first frame relatable question or visual problem; middle frames tiny escalation; last frame punchline or quiet tender reversal. One short line maximum per panel; aim to understand without caption. 4:5 carousel `1080x1350`; 9:16 short Reel reframes only when the SAME original art is preserved. Cover should read at tiny phone size; avoid text smaller than comfortably legible.
**Editorial mix:** 60% couple mini-humor, 25% warm relatable moments, 15% honest product applications. No shop link in each post; product posts 1 in 6 or less until enough audience trust.
**Account language:** English first for international audience; avoid fake claim to be US-local.
**Brand voice:** dry, casual, slightly weird, sweet without being sentimental or cliché. Examples: “The blanket is technically shared.” / “Two mugs. One missing cookie.” / “Christmas energy: 2%.” Short captions with one genuine audience question.

## 3. CREATIVE QA CHECKLIST BEFORE EVERY IMAGE, VIDEO OR POD OFFER
1. Compare original 3 reference files **side-by-side** at same subject scale. Does hair, oversized eyes, line quality, body, red/blue shirts match? If no: REJECT.
2. Is it just a situational change? If any character-model feature changed: REJECT.
3. Can a 120px thumbnail tell what happened? If no: simplify environment rather than enlarging or polishing characters.
4. Can the joke be understood in under 5 seconds? If no: cut dialogue.
5. Does print artwork remain high-contrast and legible on product; with transparencies/bleed/print safe-area tested? If no: create an appropriate technical export without restyling characters.
6. Is artwork described truthfully as **original digitally illustrated hand-drawn-style**, not falsely presented as a photographed handmade/commissioned portrait? If no: fix copy.
7. Are mockups actual Fourthwall generated images showing the exact printable artwork? If no: do not publish.
