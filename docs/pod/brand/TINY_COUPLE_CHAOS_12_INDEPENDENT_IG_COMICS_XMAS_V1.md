## Episode 01 — The Blanket Is Shared
**Category:** Everyday / evergreen · **Format:** 4-panel carousel
1. Both awkward-looking characters sit under a tiny blanket. On art: `OUR BLANKET.`
2. One edge slides over 10 cm. On art: `50 / 50`
3. A tiny rustle: the whole blanket has gradually moved to one person; other character is frozen wide-eyed. No words.
4. A second tiny emergency blanket appears only covering a toe. On art: `technically shared.`
**Caption:** "The blanket is technically shared. How much are you actually getting? 🥲"
**POD fragment:** two exposed round-eyed faces under the blanket; no microscopic labels.

## Episode 03 — The Cookie Investigation
**Category:** Christmas / snacks · **Format:** 4-panel

2. One character points to scattered crumbs leading across the table. `EVIDENCE.`

4. Camera pulls out: plate label says `FOR GUESTS`. Both hide their hands. `oh.`
**Caption:** "The gingerbread went missing. The witnesses are unreliable. 🍪"
**POD fragment:** funny double guilty face holding a gingerbread cookie.
