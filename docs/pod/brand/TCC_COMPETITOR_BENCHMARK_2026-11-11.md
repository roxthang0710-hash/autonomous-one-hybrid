**Purpose:** content and business model research; no copying of characters, scenes, dialogue, logos or protected visual styling. Existing original imperfect couple artwork remains the only character source. No named protagonists, no long-running lore.

## Editorial hypothesis
60% bite-sized couple comics, 25% standalone source art / artistic process, and up to 15% honest product/merch visuals **after shop LIVE**. While shop is COMING_SOON, replace purchase CTAs with 'opening soon' and do not claim availability.
Experimental cadence is 3–4 times/week; there is NO guaranteed posting frequency that unlocks organic reach. Track shares per reach, saves per reach, nonfollower reach, profile visits and audience geography once connected.
Content originality matters: https://about.fb.com/news/2024/10/best-practices-education-hub-creators-instagram/ ; https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-updates-algorithm-to-benefit-original-creators/819016/

## Quality principles
1. Original hand-drawn couple: dark messy bun, dark spiky hair, awkward mismatched big eyes, wobbly ink, muted cream/brick/dusty blue as seen in actual approved master art.
2. Changes allowed only in scenery, props, seasonal events and independent jokes; no polishing, vector redraw, glossy gradients, branded characters, fake handmade claims.
3. Every IG post independent; no names, no serialized plot, no personality role locked to one partner.
4. Every comic has a first-frame recognizable everyday problem and a closing visual joke. One short English line or less per panel where possible.
5. Potential POD use derives from one isolated readable artwork moment. Product gallery only uses actual Fourthwall mockups matching products.
6. Never use a competitor's individual panels, copywriting or protected characters in marketing assets.
