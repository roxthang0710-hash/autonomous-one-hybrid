### QA accept / reject checklist
- The two figures look exactly like the selected visual OPTION 3 when seen at 48px. Yellow beanie and heart headphones must remain unaltered.
- No artificial skin/hair/anthropomorphic anime features, real human clothes or additional mascot to confuse recognition.
- Episodes must feel distinguishable: at least one real emotional beat and one visual joke; no random clip-art heart wallpaper.
- All visual story panels use the same source model; no character drift in size, colors, accessories or proportions.
- English copy is brief and proofreading completed outside image generation.
- Merch designs are isolated and simplified; do not simply print the complete comic or a cream-toned marketing poster onto all products.
- Confirm product margins, print area, mockup design placement, detail legibility, and no inadvertent white rectangular backgrounds on natural/dark products.
- Third-party intellectual property not traced, no copied QuirkyQing drawings.
